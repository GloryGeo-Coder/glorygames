How to Play

  • Move with A/D or the arrow keys. On phones, hold either direction while using Jump, Dash or Shoot.
  • Collect keys to open locked gates.
  • Step on glowing pressure plates to unlock hidden bridges.
  • Space / Up / W jumps, Shift / J dashes, and K / F shoots. Hold Shoot on phones for repeated shots while ammo lasts.
  • Checkpoint flags offer safe recovery after a hit or fall. Supplies and optional ledges reward exploration.
  • Dodge snakes, spiders, bats, stone monkeys, rolling boulders, spikes, and darts.
  • Watch the glow before an enemy attack. Jump onto small enemies, dash through them or use relic shots.
  • P / Escape pauses; M opens the map. Finish each route to unlock the next. Defeat guardians to restore the Sun Seals.
  • Adjust Music and Sound effects in Pause. Sound On / Off controls both.
