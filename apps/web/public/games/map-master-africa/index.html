Your atlas journal

Atlas XP records your best result for each task, so replaying improves mastery without farming points. 100 XP: independent answer. 70 XP: answer with a hint. 50 XP: answer after a mistake.

Three stars: no mistakes or hints. Two stars: at most two mistakes. One star: another completed expedition.

Controls and map notes

Tap squares or country outlines. Drag to pan; pinch or use + / − to zoom. On a keyboard, Tab to controls; Enter or Space activates them. Within the town grid, arrow keys move between squares. On the world map, arrows pan and Enter selects the centre. Find offers a searchable country list.

Community activities use original fictional town plans and labelled practice evidence. The landform cross-section is schematic and not to scale. Provincial views use Natural Earth 1:10m boundaries; the mainland view excludes distant islands from its viewport, while the Prince Edward Islands view makes them accessible. Coloured provinces use abbreviations explained in Map key. The world atlas uses Natural Earth overview boundaries. They show countries and territories, including disputed areas, and are not a count of sovereign states. Oceania is a region containing Australia and Pacific islands. The overview is not an equal-distance or equal-area map.
