Chapter One · Save the Family Garage

Driver, garage and career progress save on this device. Story jobs use the Family Coupe or a customer’s loan car; your arcade garage stays yours.

Own the night

Start City of Legends to save the family garage through six story jobs. Or race three laps on a single street or all twelve streets. Collect cyan pickups to refill Nitro. Bumps and hazards slow you down and affect condition, then you recover. They never end your race.

Desktop: A/D or arrow keys to steer. Hold S or Down to brake. Hold Space or Shift for Nitro. P or Escape pauses; M toggles audio.

Mobile: use the steering buttons or swipe. Hold BRAKE to slow down or NITRO to boost. Landscape gives a wider view.

Race rewards buy cars and upgrades in your garage. Progress is saved on this device.
