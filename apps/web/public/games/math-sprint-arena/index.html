What will I learn?

This path practises selected Grade 4 CAPS skills: place value, whole-number calculations, patterns, money, common fractions, shapes, symmetry, length, area, mass, capacity, time, tallies and graphs. The twenty missions contain 100 Adventure activities and 100 alternate practice activities. Each set has five tasks with hints and worked explanations. It is a practice resource, rather than a complete Grade 4 course or a formal assessment.

Progress shows independent and assisted answers separately. You can repeat any unlocked Adventure mission or practise any topic in Learn mode.
