Climate Rescue Quest

Restore local habitats, connect clean-energy networks and push back the Carbon Boss.

Drag anywhere in the arena, swipe, or hold Left/Right. Keyboard: A/D or arrows. Collect green eco-items and avoid smog, smoky factories and wildfire. Shield (S) blocks one hit; Magnet (M) attracts eco-items; Freeze (F) slows falling objects. Power-ups can be replenished; leaf hearts restore a life. Bosses weaken when you collect eco-actions. Finish with at least two lives and no more than two hits for ★★, or take no hits for ★★★. P pauses. All goals and the restoration time must be met.

Complete a mission to unlock the next. Unlocked regions can be replayed.
