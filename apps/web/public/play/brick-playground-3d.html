    City Transport

    Drive your saved Vehicle Workshop buggy through this settlement. Fit a driver's seat and an open cargo bed in the workshop first.

    The buggy is wider than the four-stud road paint. Leave building setbacks and room to turn at corners. At a stop, line up with the street in either direction and hold Brake. The panel tells you when you can collect or deliver.

    Choose a second drop-off to load two crates and deliver in either order. Open Delivery round while driving to change your next stop.

    Saving a new delivery replaces the current route and starts with an empty buggy. Reloads resume at the last delivery checkpoint.

    Resident trip

    The child stays with this adult on the same journey. Families share one household slot and never create extra shop purchases.

    Daily visits repeat on the settlement clock. When all households are home, you can skip to the next departure.

    Community Market Day

    Open a spaza and serve two households from different homes. Walk inside to work the till.

    Optional: deliver crates to this shop’s frontage street. Each crate supplies two households; each purchase uses one supply. Unused stock stays with the shop between market days.

    The shopkeeper stands beside the till and takes three seconds to serve each arriving household. They use delivered stock while you build, walk or drive. Leave standing space clear. Shifts pause while menus are open or the tab is hidden.

    The first round opens now. Later rounds open at this time from tomorrow, after two purchases are complete and those customers leave the till. Resting shoppers are invited automatically. Each purchase still needs delivered stock. Menus and hidden tabs pause time; closing the game does not create sales.

            Autosaves on this device.

            Tap the plot to aim, then Place. Drag to orbit. Pinch to zoom or pan. Use Select to change a brick. On a keyboard, use arrows to aim, R to rotate, Enter to place, Tab between controls, and WASD and Space to explore. In the workshop, steer and accelerate with the joystick or WASD, and use Brake or Space to slow down. A heavier brick body accelerates more slowly. Tap the same stud again in Select to reach the seat under a cab. In Canal Crossing, choose a hinge before placing a deck. Raise the bridge for the ferry, then lower it for the buggy. In Market District, focus on a plot to build. Start a delivery at the depot, weigh the crate at the hall, then take it to the spaza. Use ramps or steps to reach the loading decks. In Settlement Builder, Transport lets you choose a pickup and one or two drop-off streets. Drive your saved workshop buggy, brake at each marker and deliver in either order. Open Delivery round to choose your next stop. In Residents, open Community Market Day and enable Use delivered stock for a spaza that needs supplies. Enable Reopen every settlement day to retain the shopkeeper and stock across daily trading rounds. Choose an opening time, review saved trading history, or pause daily reopening. Resting customers are invited automatically; when everyone is home, Skip to next opening advances the settlement clock. Plan stock delivery selects its frontage; each delivered crate supplies two household purchases. Add a shopkeeper before opening the stocked market to serve arrivals while you build or drive. Leave space beside the till and use Pause shopkeeper to suspend the shift. Use Last stop to recover, or Build to clear an obstruction. Use City map to name blocks, choose land use and lay streets. Choose Build here or Walk here to apply the map. Open Plans for homes, offices, a hall or a park. Choose Residents to assign a home and spaza. Follow Lebo to the spaza or Naledi to the community hall. Both trips run together. Reconnect missing streets or move a brick to clear the entrance. In Flat Above the Shop, collect the parcel at the counter and walk it upstairs. Aim a repair to preview one possible route. In Free Build, Market District and Flat Above the Shop, Plans saves a whole plot or the structure around a selected brick. Preview a copy, tap its new starting stud, rotate it and choose Place copy. For Market Freight, fit a driver’s seat and an open cargo bed in the workshop. Dispatch to market and brake inside each marked bay to handle the crates.

            In Settlement Builder, open Town trail on the city map to name a walk and choose two to five doorway stops. Start the walk and step inside each landmark to stamp your passport.
