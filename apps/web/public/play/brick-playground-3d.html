Autosaves on this device.

Tap the plot to aim, then Place. Drag to orbit. Pinch to zoom or pan. Use Select to change a brick. On a keyboard, use arrows to aim, R to rotate, Enter to place, Tab between controls, and WASD and Space to explore. In the workshop, steer and accelerate with the joystick or WASD, and use Brake or Space to slow down. A heavier brick body accelerates more slowly. Tap the same stud again in Select to reach the seat under a cab. In Canal Crossing, choose a hinge before placing a deck. Raise the bridge for the ferry, then lower it for the buggy. In Market District, focus on a plot to build. Start a delivery at the depot, weigh the crate at the hall, then take it to the spaza. Use ramps or steps to reach the loading decks. In Free Build and Market District, Plans saves a whole plot or the structure around a selected brick. Preview a copy, tap its new starting stud, rotate it and choose Place copy. For Market Freight, fit a driver’s seat and an open cargo bed in the workshop. Dispatch to market and brake inside each marked bay to handle the crates.
