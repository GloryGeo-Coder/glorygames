# Map Master — Grade 4 and Grade 5 practice coverage

The practice path keeps the original five Map Academy missions first, then applies those map tools in field investigations. Term labels identify the source topic; this game path is not an annual school timetable, a complete teaching programme or an official assessment. Grade 5 can start independently. All questions are original. Mistakes produce guidance and another attempt, with learner-controlled continuation.

## Evidence and geographic scope

- Community, farming, water, weather, climate-profile and mine-community records are fictional examples, visibly identified in their evidence cards. They are not real observations or production rankings.
- The landform diagram is an original schematic without a measured elevation scale. The letters A–D agree with its evidence table. It cannot establish real mountain heights.
- Province questions locate provincial outlines. Physical-feature questions explicitly distinguish a province from a mountain outline or mine site. No fictional river or mineral-deposit geometry is overlaid on a real map.
- Source geographic labels, rendering and selection share longitude/latitude transforms. Overview pixels are not ground distances. Prince Edward Islands remain in the provincial source and are available through their dedicated regional view.
- Capitals and general facts are stable descriptive information, avoiding current population or production totals. Natural Earth is an overview source, not a cadastral/legal boundary authority.
