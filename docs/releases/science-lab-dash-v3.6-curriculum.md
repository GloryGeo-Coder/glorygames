## Models and quantitative assumptions

- Carbon-12 and carbon-14 share six protons but have six/eight neutrons. Nitrogen-14 shares the latter mass number while having a different proton count. Z counts protons and A counts protons plus neutrons; the displayed records are neutral. Atomic marker regions are schematic, not scale models or electron trajectories.
- Cl⁻ retains seventeen protons and gains an electron, giving eighteen electrons and charge −1. An eighteen-proton record is argon, not chloride. No ion-making procedure is supplied.
- The isotope-average activity uses an invented 75:25 sample and approximate relative isotope masses 35/37, giving 35.5. It does not claim exact natural abundances or chlorine’s exact standard atomic weight. Relative atomic mass is dimensionless; individual isotope mass numbers remain integers.
- The neon ground-state record 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ contains ten electrons. Each s subshell has capacity two, while 2p has capacity six. Eight electrons in the second shell do not mean eight in 2p. This selected activity reads subshell occupancy; it is not a complete orbital/Aufbau assessment through Z = 20.
- One H₂ single covalent bond is represented by a shared pair of electrons. Ionic transfer/lattice and metallic delocalisation records are distinguished from it. The diagrams are introductory bonding representations, not actual electron paths or a full quantum model.
- MgCl₂ is a simplest neutral ratio in an extended ionic solid: one Mg²⁺ plus two Cl⁻. Proposed MgCl and Mg₂Cl ratios fail that specified charge balance. The illustrated ion group is not a discrete solid-state molecule.
- The structure comparison distinguishes diamond’s bound covalent-network electrons, graphite’s delocalised electrons and copper’s metallic carriers. Graphite’s conducting record is explicitly along its layers; it does not assert direction-independent conductivity. Structure motifs are qualitative rather than exact crystal geometry.
- At fixed normal pressure, ordinary pure-water boiling can have a temperature plateau while supplied energy helps overcome intermolecular attractions. The internal O–H covalent bonds and H₂O identity remain. Six unchanged molecules are retained in the valid diagram. Enlarged or split-molecule records are deliberately incorrect proposals.
- Mass records start with the same apparatus-plus-contents mass of 120 g. The closed record retains 120 g on the balance. The valid open record retains 116 g and accounts for 4 g outside as escaped gas. The destroyed-mass proposal omits 4 g and fails conservation. These are supplied idealised records, not an experiment or a gas-production procedure.
- The [BIPM SI definition of the mole](https://www.bipm.org/en/si-base-units/mole) fixes the Avogadro constant at 6.02214076 × 10²³ mol⁻¹. Half a mole of water contains 3.01107038 × 10²³ H₂O molecules; the total atom count is three times the molecule count. The entity is always specified. Amount bars are scaled illustrations, not literal particle images.
- Water molar mass uses supplied rounded atomic molar masses H = 1 and O = 16 g/mol, giving 18 g/mol. This is not the precise physical molar mass. Relative molecular mass is dimensionless; molar mass carries g/mol. The mass-to-amount activity uses the same rounded 18 g/mol with 9 g water to obtain 0.5 mol.
- The concentration record uses 0.20 mol NaCl in 500 mL of final solution: 0.500 dm³, giving 0.40 mol/dm³. The volume is the final solution volume, not automatically the starting solvent volume. The NaCl amount is a solute formula amount, not a claim that dissolved NaCl remains as discrete molecules.
- The ideal-yield record assumes complete conversion of 4 g H₂ with oxygen in excess and rounded molar masses M(H₂) = 2 and M(H₂O) = 18 g/mol. The balanced 2 H₂ + O₂ → 2 H₂O ratio gives 2 mol water and 36 g ideal yield. Actual yields may be lower. No combustion, gas-handling or reaction procedure is supplied.
- The finale uses c = 0.20 mol/dm³, V = 250 mL = 0.250 dm³ and supplied M(NaCl) = 58.5 g/mol. It obtains n = 0.050 mol and m = 2.925 g. Full arithmetic values are kept for transparency, not as a significant-figure assessment or real measurement-precision claim. The alternatives miss the same final-volume concentration target. This is an audit of proposed records, not a laboratory preparation recipe.

## Learning-path completion

Every activity retains untimed prediction, distinct recorded comparisons, a tested working setting, supported explanation and application. Hints, reference choices, trial history and answer order remain recoverable. The first-twenty element guide supports the isotope archive and neon investigation and their replays. Both graphite and copper can satisfy the conductor brief.

Grade 10 opens after the thirty earlier adventure missions. Completing the finale resolves the academy story and gives thirty-five adventure missions and 105 recorded investigations across seven five-mission paths. The original twenty-mission science dash is retained separately. Existing lesson/story/audio definitions and save schema version 1 are preserved. Final website text describes the selected curriculum scope and both game modes accurately, without claiming a complete school syllabus.
