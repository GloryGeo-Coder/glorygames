# BioQuest Grade 10: The Living Archive

The final planned grade adds five missions and 15 investigations. BioQuest now offers seven independently selectable Grades 4–10 paths, 35 missions and 105 investigations. Nala remains the female explorer, with Pip her drone companion. Finishing Grade 10 celebrates its Living Archive chapter, not completion of unplayed earlier grades.

## Selected curriculum coverage

- Molecular Evidence Lab: biomolecule roles, interpreting enzyme-temperature data and identifying controlled variables.
- Membrane Exchange: diffusion, osmosis, active transport and predictions from model conditions.
- Growth and Repair: mitosis sequence, chromosome-number continuity and cell differentiation.
- Plant Transport Station: xylem/phloem, water movement and water-uptake evidence without equating it exactly to transpiration.
- The Deep-Time Archive: fossilisation, relative ages in undisturbed strata and evidence-informed habitat protection.

This is selected CAPS learning support, not a complete syllabus, practical programme or examination replacement. Full animal anatomy/physiology, detailed classification, geological timescale coverage, all biochemical content and other omitted subtopics remain outside this increment. Data examples are fictional teaching models. No personal questions, health advice, accounts or personal-data collection are added.
