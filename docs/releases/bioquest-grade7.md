# BioQuest Grade 7: The biodiversity expedition

Adds five independent, untimed missions (15 investigations), taking the game to 20 missions across Grades 4–7. Female explorer Nala and her drone Pip build a community biodiversity atlas through sorting, evidence-based choices and a reproduction sequence. Existing Grade 4–6 mission data and the storage key are preserved. Grade selection and checkpoints remain independent; no account or personal information is requested.

## Curriculum scope

Checked against the Department of Basic Education [2026 Natural Sciences Grade 7 ATP](https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_NS_Grade%207.pdf?ver=2026-01-20-202040-030), Life and Living, pages 1–3, on 7 November 2026. This is a selected learning path, not full curriculum or assessment coverage.

- The Living Planet: Earth systems, habitat suitability and fair seedling comparisons.
- The Discovery Archive: shared features, the curriculum's five-kingdom model and variation within a species.
- The Wildlife Evidence Trail: familiar vertebrate groups, arthropods and molluscs, responsible observation.
- The Seed and Spore Garden: ferns, cycads and flowering plants, seed leaves and seasonal evidence.
- The Pollination Partnership: flower structures, pollination versus fertilisation, seed formation and pollination diversity.

Human reproduction, detailed reproductive anatomy, full life-process teaching, comprehensive taxonomic ranks, extended investigations and assessment are not included. These missions do not replace teacher-led lessons. The five-kingdom model is identified as a teaching model rather than the only current classification system. Scientific wording distinguishes pollination from fertilisation, spores from seeds, and wind-pollinated maize from animal-pollinated flowers. No simplistic single-gene claims about human variation are made.

## Integration and validation

The game page, catalog, manifest, script loading and four-grade picker reflect the selected scope. Five original synthesized melodies accompany Grade 7, without external audio dependencies. Repeated and stale answer/advance callbacks are guarded across screen changes. No deployment or merge is part of this change.

Automated checks cover complete campaigns, grade switching, checkpoint migration/resume/replay, storage failures, invalid saves and repeated/interrupted input. Dedicated CI runs production build and real Chromium checks at narrow phone, portrait, landscape and desktop sizes and retains screenshots. Passing Node simulations is not visual or physical-device verification. Inspect actual CI results and screenshots for the final commit; physical-device testing remains outstanding.
