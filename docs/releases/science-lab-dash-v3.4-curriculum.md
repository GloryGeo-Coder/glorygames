## Model assumptions and limits

- The carbon-12 model shows six protons, six neutrons and six electrons. Proton count identifies the element; equal proton/electron counts establish neutrality. Neutron counts can vary between isotopes. The positive carbon ion and neutral nitrogen alternatives distinguish these ideas without treating every neutral atom as carbon. The first-twenty guide is available in this investigation and its replay. Atomic regions, distances and sizes are schematic; the boundary is not an electron orbit.
- The nucleus record compares deliberately incorrect labels with positive protons and uncharged neutrons in the nucleus, and negative electrons in the surrounding region. Marker shapes are teaching symbols, not solid objects seen inside an atom.
- O₂ is a molecule of an element; H₂O is a molecular compound. The pure samples contain only one substance each. The mixture contains distinct O₂ and CO₂ molecules. Bond lines join atoms within a molecule, not separate molecules. These examples do not imply that every compound is molecular; ionic structures are outside this increment.
- H₂O has two H atoms per O atom; H₂O₂ is hydrogen peroxide, a different compound. The activity interprets supplied formula records and includes no chemical handling procedure. Purity is not a drinking-water safety claim.
- State diagrams retain twelve equal-size markers. A solid vibrates around fixed positions, a liquid has close mobile particles and a gas has widely separated mobile particles. The particle-model gaps are empty, not filled with additional air. Position and animation are qualitative rather than physical trajectories.
- Diffusion snapshots retain twelve tracer markers. Other particles of the medium are omitted explicitly. The gas example spreads farther than the liquid example after the same illustrative interval. Random motion produces net spreading from the concentrated region; individual motion does not stop at uniform concentration. The data are not measured diffusion rates or a universal ranking independent of conditions.
- The valid freezing record retains six H₂O molecules and the same mass in a more open, larger-volume ordinary-ice structure. Ordinary ice is less dense than liquid water and can float; water is an exception to the usual school solid/liquid density comparison. Smaller-volume and changed-identity records are proposals to reject, not simulated physical outcomes. The diagram does not represent exact crystalline geometry or measured volumes.
- Cargo values are invented: A = 100 g / 50 cm³ = 2 g/cm³, B = 80 g / 100 cm³ = 0.8 g/cm³, C = 200 g / 100 cm³ = 2 g/cm³. Comparing A and C prevents ranking density by mass alone. The brief requires below 1 g/cm³; it is not a real lift rating or a complete buoyancy model.
- The typical metal-ball model keeps twelve equal-size atom markers. Average spacing and the exaggerated ball diameter change while the ring stays unchanged. Reference and cooler conditions both fit; the warmer condition does not. The model does not assert identical expansion for all materials or give apparatus temperatures.
- The chamber has fixed volume and temperature. Six, twelve and eighteen chamber markers correspond to illustrative pressure indices 1, 2 and 3. Eighteen markers remain accounted for across supply and chamber. Wall collisions explain pressure, and the target is the middle index rather than maximum pressure. The index is not a real operating limit. Heating-dependent pressure and quantitative gas laws are outside this activity.
- Melting and boiling retain H₂O identity. The carbon + oxygen → carbon dioxide record changes bonding while retaining one C and two O atoms. The activity identifies starting reactants and formed products; neither bubbles nor temperature change alone is presented as universal proof of reaction.
- The water-formation ledger starts with two H₂ molecules and one O₂ molecule and ends with two H₂O molecules. Four H and two O atoms remain, while molecule counts change. Missing-hydrogen and invented-carbon product proposals are explicitly invalid. This is diagram interpretation, not formal equation balancing or a laboratory procedure.
- Virtual energy-driven decomposition reverses those bonded groups: two H₂O become two H₂ and one O₂. Filtration and evaporation leave water’s identity intact. The example distinguishes physical mixture separation from chemical decomposition, without electricity, heating, gas-collection or reaction instructions.

## Learning and continuity

Every investigation retains prediction, at least two distinct recorded settings, a tested working result, explanation and application. Progressive hints and retries remain untimed. Reference-card taps do not earn score. Alternative working choices are accepted where the brief allows them.

Grade 8 unlocks after the twenty Grade 4–7 missions. Completing the new path gives twenty-five adventure missions and seventy-five saved investigations. The original twenty-mission dash remains available separately. Earlier definitions and music arrangements remain unchanged; existing save schema version 1 is retained. Grade 9–10 paths and final game-page editorial updates remain later increments.
