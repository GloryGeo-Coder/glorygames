# Lost Jungle v2.6 — Guardians on every route

Every one of the 25 routes ends in a guardian encounter. Nineteen new named encounters reuse the existing animated Monkey King, Spider Queen and Sun Guardian families, with family-specific movement and later double or three-shot patterns. The original six seal guardian encounters retain their original names, health and behaviour. Ari must defeat the guardian as well as collect the required relics and keys, and activate any required pressure plate, before the exit opens.

Boss health grows across the new encounters. The final approach retains its ammunition, shield pickup and checkpoint, and dashing can defeat a guardian if the player runs out of ammunition. The expedition map, on-screen mission and guardian health bar show the encounter name. The existing save format, unlocked levels, score, sound settings, soundtrack and artwork remain unchanged.

The game metadata, information-page story and asset cache versions are updated. Tests cover the 25 guardian gates, dash recovery with no ammunition, original routes, scores, saves, controls and audio. A browser workflow exercises representative guardian fights at phone, landscape and desktop sizes.
